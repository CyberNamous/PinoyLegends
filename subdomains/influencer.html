Content Creator Program

Make the game, on camera

The Pinoy Legends Content Creator Program supports creators making real, long-form coverage of the game — gameplay, guides, and commentary that gives viewers an honest look at what we're building.

Program requirements

What qualifies

These requirements apply to every submission, regardless of tier.

  • 2-minute minimum runtime. Submitted videos must run at least 2 minutes long.
  • Short-form is excluded. 15-second clips, Shorts, Reels, and Stories do not qualify for the program.
  • Platforms. Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok follower counts all count toward eligibility.
  • Applications only through the form. All submissions are filtered strictly through the official Google Form below — no other channel is reviewed.

Creator tiers

Uprising & Prestige

Your tier is based on your largest single-platform following at the time you apply.
